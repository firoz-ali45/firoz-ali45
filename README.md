## 👋 About Me

I'm **Firoz Ali**, an operations and technology professional working at the intersection of **business operations, ERP systems, data analytics, and AI-assisted software development**.

My approach is practical: understand the workflow, identify friction, model the data, automate repeatable work, and verify the result.

- 🏢 **Operations & ERP:** inventory, warehouse processes, multi-branch operations, and Odoo workflows.
- 📊 **Data & BI:** operational reporting, dashboards, cost visibility, and decision support.
- 🔌 **Integration & automation:** APIs, structured data, and repeatable business workflows.
- 🧠 **AI-assisted engineering:** using AI coding tools to accelerate development while checking quality and correctness.
- 🛡️ **Engineering mindset:** security, testing, reliability, documentation, and maintainability.

> **My north star:** Make complex operations easier to see, easier to run, and easier to improve.
