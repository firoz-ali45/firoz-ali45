## 👋 About Me

I'm **Firoz Ali**, an operations and technology professional focused on the space where **business operations, ERP, data analytics, and AI-assisted development** meet.

My approach is grounded in real workflows: understand the process, clarify the data, reduce manual effort, measure the result, and build systems that are easier to maintain.

- **ERP & operations:** inventory, warehouse workflows, multi-branch processes, and Odoo.
- **Analytics & reporting:** dashboards, operational metrics, cost visibility, and decision support.
- **Automation & integration:** APIs, structured data, and repeatable business workflows.
- **Application development:** AI-assisted prototyping with attention to testing, security, and reliability.
- **Engineering growth:** improving architecture, DevOps, documentation, and production practices.

> **My mission:** Make business operations easier to understand, easier to run, and easier to improve.
